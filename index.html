Things people ask me

  

    
Are you a dealer or a salesperson?
No. No dealer commissions, and I never touch your money. You buy from the seller — I find and negotiate.

    
How fast will I get my 3 picks?
Depends on rarity. Common cars turn up fast; one exact trim/colour takes longer. I'll be upfront if your budget or timeline doesn't fit.

    
What if I don't like any of the 3?
Tell me why and I'll search again. If you picked “exact car only,” I won't pad the list with near-misses.

    
What if you can't beat the price?
I'll tell you. Sometimes it's already a fair price — then the answer is just “buy that one.” Fee is still $495 min / $1,295 cap on documented savings.

    
Can you just look at a car I already found?
Yes — paste the link in the form. I'll check it against similar sales and can negotiate that car directly.
